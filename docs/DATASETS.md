# Dataset provenance

No restricted or production PCAP is bundled. The committed scenarios are safe metadata-only acceptance inputs generated by `traffic-gen/generators/generate_scenarios.py`; they are not benchmarks.

For every final PCAP/lab input, add source URL, usage terms, filename, SHA-256, exact threat labels, capture direction, preprocessing command, and limitations to `experiments/datasets.yml`. Use only permitted public research or lab-generated traffic. Do not mark a class as covered until its labels have been inspected in the exact input.

Suggested lab proof inputs are the mechanisms named in the PS: benign iperf3/Ostinato/TRex traffic; controlled hping3 SYN/UDP behavior; sandboxed C2 timing emitter; published DGA lists; dnscat2/iodine in an isolated lab; and controlled upload-volume scenarios. Capture only with authorization and do not redistribute prohibited data.

The committed acceptance suite is generated by `make evaluate` and writes [results.md](../eval/results.md) and `results.json`. It is scenario-level coverage only, intentionally not an inflated F1 claim.
